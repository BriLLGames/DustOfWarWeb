The concept

A world that argues back

Dust of War is a global military-political strategy game. You don't just move divisions on a map — you negotiate, threaten, bluff, and bargain with governments that are meant to actually respond to you.

Most strategy games let you do anything you want and barely push back. Dust of War is being built around the opposite idea: a diplomatic layer that reacts to the player in real time, a front line that can genuinely shift under your feet, and a toolset that lets the community build alongside the developer instead of waiting on them.

It's a solo project, still early in development, and the description below is the current plan rather than a finished feature list — some of this will change as the game takes shape.

Everything on this page describes planned or in-progress features and may change before release.
Three pillars

What Dust of War is built around

◆

LLM-driven diplomacy

Nations respond to the player through a language model instead of a fixed script — generating events, diplomatic offers, and reactions on the fly, in real time rather than turn by turn. The goal is to run this on free-tier models so playing doesn't come with an ongoing subscription cost, with the option to plug in stronger models later for anyone who wants it. A standard scripted AI will also be available as an alternative for players who prefer it or don't want to rely on an LLM at all.

▲

Dynamic territory control

Front lines and borders are meant to shift as a direct result of what happens in the world, not snap between fixed regions. This kind of system is demanding to build and to run, so a range of quality and performance presets is planned — with the long-term goal of the game being playable even on mobile hardware.

✚

Built-in modding & Workshop

Custom maps, scenarios, new countries, and adjusted game parameters — created from inside the game itself, without needing to learn a separate modding toolchain. Finished mods will be publishable straight to an in-game Workshop, so other players can pick them up in a couple of clicks.
