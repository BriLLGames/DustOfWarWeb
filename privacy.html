1.Who we are

Dust of War is developed and operated by BrillGames, an independent, one-person game studio ("we", "us", "the developer"). This policy explains what information is collected through the Dust of War game client, its account/login system, its multiplayer and chat features, and this website, and what is done with that information.

2.Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Dust of War game and this website. It does not cover third-party platforms you may use to reach us or play the game, such as Steam, Discord, Telegram, or YouTube — those services have their own privacy policies, and your use of them is governed by those policies, not this one.

3.Information we collect

Account information

Creating a Dust of War account requires an email address and a password. We do not require your real name, phone number, or physical address to create or use an account.

Age information

We do not currently collect your age or date of birth. If a future mobile release includes optional advertising, we may ask for age information at that point solely to comply with age-appropriate advertising rules, and only with clear notice and, where required, your consent. This policy will be updated before that happens.

Gameplay and account activity

This includes your in-game username, save data, progress, settings, and actions relevant to multiplayer sessions (for example, moves, diplomatic actions, and match history).

Chat and diplomatic messages

Any text you send through in-game chat or diplomatic interactions — whether to another player or to the game's AI system — is processed in order to make that feature work, and may be logged for moderation and abuse-prevention purposes. See Section 6 for more detail.

Technical and device information

Like most online games, we automatically receive some technical information when you play online or use the website: IP address, general device/OS information, and crash or error logs. This is used for security, anti-cheat, and bug-fixing purposes, not for building an advertising profile.

Payment information

If Dust of War is sold through a platform such as Steam, your purchase is handled entirely by that platform. We do not receive or store your card number or full payment details — at most, we may see that a purchase or refund occurred, if the platform provides that information to developers.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your full legal name, home address, government ID, or phone number to use the game.

4.How we use information

  • To create and maintain your account and let you log in.
  • To run core gameplay features, including multiplayer sessions, diplomacy, and chat.
  • To generate AI-driven diplomatic responses and events (see Section 5).
  • To detect cheating, abuse, and violations of the Terms of Use, including processing player reports.
  • To diagnose crashes, bugs, and performance issues.
  • To communicate with you about your account, security notices, or major changes to the game or this policy.
  • To meet legal obligations where applicable.

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell personal information to data brokers or third parties.

5.AI processing & third-party AI providers

Dust of War's diplomacy system is powered by large language models. This is one of the most important things to understand about how your data moves:

  • Cloud AI mode (default in most cases): when a diplomatic exchange or AI-driven event is generated, the relevant in-game text (for example, your negotiation message or the current game state needed to generate a response) is sent to OpenRouter, a third-party service that routes requests to various underlying AI model providers. That text is processed by OpenRouter and the model provider it routes to in order to generate a response, subject to OpenRouter's own privacy policy and the policies of whichever underlying model is used. We do not control how those third parties retain or process data beyond what their own policies state, and we encourage you to review them.
  • Local AI mode (optional): where supported by your hardware, Dust of War is intended to let you run a language model locally on your own device instead of using the cloud service. In this mode, diplomatic text is processed on your machine and is not sent to us or to OpenRouter.
  • Scripted AI mode: a traditional, non-LLM scripted AI is also planned as an alternative for players who would rather not use either cloud or local language models at all.

We recommend avoiding sharing personal or sensitive information in chat or diplomatic text, since — depending on the mode you choose — it may be transmitted to a third-party AI provider for processing.

6.Multiplayer, chat & communication

Dust of War includes multiplayer sessions and in-game chat. Anything you send in a shared chat or diplomatic channel can be seen by the other participants in that session. Messages may be logged for a limited time to allow moderation, to investigate reports of abuse, and to enforce the Terms of Use.

We aim to encrypt in-transit communications (such as chat and account traffic) between the game client and our servers as part of ongoing development. This is a goal we are actively working toward rather than something we can currently guarantee is fully implemented across every feature — this section will be updated as encryption is completed and verified.

7.Cookies & local storage (website)

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It may use minimal local storage in your browser to remember simple preferences, such as your chosen language (English/Russian). No analytics or advertising scripts are embedded on this site at this time; if that changes, this policy will be updated first.

8.Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • OpenRouter and underlying AI model providers — to generate AI-driven diplomacy and events, as described in Section 5.
  • Distribution platforms (for example, Steam) — where they handle your purchase, license, or reviews, subject to their own privacy policies.
  • Service providers who help operate infrastructure (for example, hosting or backend services), under obligations to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.
  • Authorities, where required by law, to investigate fraud, security incidents, or as otherwise legally compelled.

9.Data retention

We keep account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active. Chat and moderation logs are kept only as long as reasonably needed to review reports and enforce the Terms of Use, after which they are deleted or anonymized. If you delete your account (Section 11), we will remove or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain limited records for legal, security, or anti-abuse purposes.

10.Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 16. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

11.Reporting & account deletion

Dust of War includes an in-game tool to report other players for abusive or rule-breaking behavior. Reports are used solely for moderation and enforcement of the Terms of Use.

You can delete your account at any time from the game's account settings, or by contacting us directly. Deleting your account removes your login credentials and personal profile data; some non-identifying gameplay statistics or records needed for fraud prevention or legal compliance may be retained in an anonymized form.

12.Children's privacy

Dust of War is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country). If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will delete it. If age-based advertising is introduced on mobile in the future, it will be implemented in line with applicable children's privacy laws, with clear notice in advance.

13.Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting the personal data we ask for in the first place. However, no online service can guarantee perfect security, and we cannot promise that data will never be exposed as a result of circumstances outside our reasonable control.

14.International data transfers

Because Dust of War is played over the internet and relies on third-party services (including AI providers and hosting infrastructure) that may operate in different countries, your information may be processed outside your country of residence. Where this happens, we rely on those providers' own safeguards and compliance measures.

15.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Dust of War develops — for example, when new features like local AI, mobile advertising, or additional payment options are introduced. Material changes will be highlighted on this page, and the "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

16.Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to brillgamesss@gmail.com, or through our Telegram or Discord community channels for general inquiries.
